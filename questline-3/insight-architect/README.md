# Insight Architect

This notebook performs exploratory data analysis on the cleaned student performance dataset and highlights patterns using several meaningful visualizations.

## Visualizations included
- Histogram of attendance distribution
- Bar chart of pass/fail counts
- Box plot for score distribution across subjects
- Scatter plot of attendance vs. math score
- Heatmap of numeric feature correlations
- Bar chart of average scores by gender

## Key findings
1. Attendance values are concentrated in the high range, suggesting most students are attending regularly.
2. Most students in the sample passed, indicating a generally positive academic outcome.
3. Math and Science show slightly greater score variability than English.
4. Attendance is positively associated with math performance.
5. Subject scores and attendance are moderately correlated, supporting the idea that consistent engagement supports learning.
6. Gender does not show a strong difference in average performance in this dataset.
7. Overall, stronger engagement and higher scores align with pass outcomes.

These visualizations help uncover important patterns in student performance and support a quick, interpretable EDA workflow.
